What is CodeMove Kenya?

It is a learning space built for Kenyan learners who want to understand how technology works by making things with it. You begin with the basics, build real projects such as web pages and Arduino robots, and your teacher can guide the class through the lessons step by step. No earlier experience is needed.

  • Short lessons
  • Learn by doing
  • Built-in code editor
  • Quizzes and badges
  • Teacher-led pace

Questions people ask

Who is CodeMove Kenya for?

Anyone who wants to learn coding and robotics from the beginning. Schools, clubs and teachers can also use it to run a class.

Do I need to know anything first?

No. The first lessons explain the basics, and each lesson builds on the one before.

Do I need special equipment?

The coding lessons only need a phone or computer with internet. The robotics courses use the SmartBrains Arduino kit, and you can begin by simulating circuits online.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The platform is designed to work on phones, tablets and computers.

Why can't I see some lessons yet?

Your teacher decides when each lesson opens, and may give your class a short code to unlock it. This keeps everyone learning at the same pace.

How do I get started?

Press “Start learning”, then sign up with your email or Google account. Teachers can contact us to set up a class.

Ready to start?

Create your account, open your first lesson and build something today. Teachers and schools are welcome to get in touch.
